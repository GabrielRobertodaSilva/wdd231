Official Voting Order on the Electronic Voting Machine

  1. Federal Deputy: 4 dígitos
  2. State / District Deputy: 5 dígitos
  3. Senator: 3 dígitos
  4. Governor: 2 dígitos
  5. President: 2 dígitos

Important Tips

You are permitted to bring a paper cheat sheet or consult your digital cheat sheet on the app before entering the voting booth.
